TikTok accounts

Clips post to every connected account at once.

    TikTok connects whichever account is logged in at tiktok.com. To add another account, log out there first (or connect from an incognito window), then click Connect again — repeat per account.

    Publishing

    With auto-post on, a finished job posts every rendered clip to all connected TikTok accounts the moment rendering completes. Unaudited TikTok apps can only post SELF_ONLY and are limited to a handful of posts per account per day.

    Drafts land in each account's TikTok app, ready to post with one tap and any privacy -- the mode that works before your app is audited.
